STG Outage & Capital Overhauling

OEM-GRADE ALIGNMENT

Our flagship competence lies in planned capital turnarounds and forced emergency outages for Steam Turbine Generator (STG) sets. From 10 MW industrial extraction turbines in captive power plants to 800 MW supercritical multi-cylinder utility sets, we execute complete top casing lifts, rotor extraction, NDT crack detection, diaphragm alignment, and dynamic balancing.

Casing & Diaphragms

  • Top half casing lifting with synchronized dial indicators
  • Diaphragm half removal, grit cleaning & crack inspection
  • Axial and radial clearance logging (lead wire & feeler gauges)
  • Nozzle carrier and gland labyrinth seal replacement

Rotor & Blading

  • Rotor extraction with specialized lifting balance beams
  • Complete rotor runout check (radial, axial, journal eccentricity)
  • 100% NDT inspection: Dye Penetrant (DPT) & Magnetic Particle (MPT)
  • Low-speed and trim dynamic balancing to ISO 1940 Grade G2.5

Bearings & Alignment

  • Journal tilt-pad and thrust bearing inspection & scraping
  • Bearing oil baffle clearance verification to avoid oil leaks
  • Sub-micron laser shaft alignment across couplings
  • Pedestal expansion check, catenary curve matching & cold float
MAKES HANDLED: TOSHIBA (660 MW & 800 MW SUPERCRITICAL), BHEL, SIEMENS KWU, GE, ALSTOM, DOOSAN, TRIVENI

TDBFP & BFPT Overhauling

HIGH PRESSURE EXPERTS

Boiler feed pumps (TDBFP & MDBFP) operate under harsh hydraulic forces, high temperatures, and extreme discharge pressures up to 220 bar. Failure of a feed pump trips the entire unit. We deliver precision mechanical overhauls of the multi-stage barrel pump, internal cartridge replacement, and the drive steam turbine (BFPT).

Drive Steam Turbine (BFPT)

  • Turbine casing opening, rotor extraction & blade cleaning
  • Emergency stop & governor control valve reconditioning
  • Overspeed mechanical trip bolt testing & calibration
  • Drive shaft coupling runout and laser alignment

Barrel & Inner Cartridge

  • Hydraulic torque tensioning for barrel head cover removal
  • Precision extraction of inner pump bundle cartridge
  • Impeller wear ring clearance restoration (gap 0.30 - 0.45 mm)
  • Diffuser passage descaling and cavitation inspection

Balancing & Mechanical Seals

  • Balancing drum / disc refacing & blue contact matching
  • Mechanical seal cartridge replacement & flush line overhaul
  • Hydrodynamic journal & Kingsbury thrust bearing renewal
  • Pre-commissioning solo run and coupled performance test
PUMP MAKES: BHEL, SULZER, KSB MULTITEC, WEIR, FLOWSERVE, EBARA

Critical High-Pressure Valve Reconditioning

ZERO-LEAK ASSURANCE

Main steam and reheat valves are the ultimate safety barriers between high pressure boilers and turbines. Steam passing through defective seats causes severe thermal loss and loss of megawatts. We perform in-situ diamond lapping, spindle straightening, actuator overhauling, and nitrogen leak decay testing.

In-Situ Seat Lapping

  • Portable diamond lapping heads mounted directly on valve bonnets
  • Stellite face resurfacing and micro-crack blending
  • Prussian blue contact check verifying >95% continuous contact
  • Seat ring concentricity and seat angle verification

Spindles & Packings

  • Spindle runout measurement on precision V-blocks
  • Hard chrome & stelliting repair of scored stems
  • Live-loaded die-formed flexible graphite packings installation
  • Precise torque calibration to prevent stem binding

Actuators & Pressure Testing

  • Hydraulic servo cylinder overhaul & seal replacement
  • Rack, pinion and trip link mechanism calibration
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing to 1.5x design pressure
  • High-pressure nitrogen seat decay leak verification
VALVE TYPES: MSV, ESV, CV, RSV, IV, HP/LP BYPASS, SAFETY VALVES
