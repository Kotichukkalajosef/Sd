STG Outage & Capital Overhauling

OEM-GRADE ALIGNMENT

Our flagship competence lies in planned capital turnarounds and forced emergency outages for Steam Turbine Generator (STG) sets. From 10 MW industrial extraction turbines in captive power plants to 800 MW supercritical multi-cylinder utility sets, we execute complete top casing lifts, rotor extraction, NDT crack detection, diaphragm alignment, and dynamic balancing.

Casing & Diaphragms

  • Top half casing lifting with synchronized dial indicators
  • Diaphragm half removal, grit cleaning & crack inspection
  • Axial and radial clearance logging (lead wire & feeler gauges)
  • Nozzle carrier and gland labyrinth seal replacement

Rotor & Blading

  • Rotor extraction with specialized lifting balance beams
  • Complete rotor runout check (radial, axial, journal eccentricity)
  • 100% NDT inspection: Dye Penetrant (DPT) & Magnetic Particle (MPT)
  • Low-speed and trim dynamic balancing to ISO 1940 Grade G2.5

Bearings & Alignment

  • Journal tilt-pad and thrust bearing inspection & scraping
  • Bearing oil baffle clearance verification to avoid oil leaks
  • Sub-micron laser shaft alignment across couplings
  • Pedestal expansion check, catenary curve matching & cold float
MAKES HANDLED: TOSHIBA (660 MW & 800 MW SUPERCRITICAL), BHEL, SIEMENS KWU, GE, ALSTOM, DOOSAN, TRIVENI

O&M (Operation and Maintenance)

24/7 PLANT RELIABILITY

SD Enterprises provides full-scope Operation and Maintenance (O&M) services for thermal power utilities, captive power plants (CPP), and industrial co-generation units. Our experienced engineers and certified technicians deliver round-the-clock shift operation, online health diagnostics, predictive maintenance, and balance-of-plant (BOP) systems upkeep to maximize uptime and optimize plant heat rates.

24/7 Plant Shift Operations

  • Round-the-clock shift supervision and parameter log-sheet control
  • Turbine, boiler, and balance of plant (BOP) regular rounds and health audits
  • Shift handover management and PTW (Permit to Work) safety clearance
  • Operational startup, synchronization, load ramping & controlled shutdowns

Preventive & Predictive Maintenance

  • Scheduled preventive servicing of rotating equipment, pumps & compressors
  • Online FFT vibration tracking and bearing temperature anomaly analysis
  • Lube oil filtration, chemical analysis, viscosity & moisture tests
  • Dynamic trim balancing and laser shaft re-alignment on operating units

Balance of Plant (BOP) Upkeep

  • Cooling water pumps, CW towers, compressors & chemical dosing systems
  • High-pressure feedwater pump (TDBFP/MDBFP) routine servicing & seal renewals
  • Critical steam stop & control valve health checks and packing adjustments
  • Rapid emergency troubleshooting and breakdown recovery squads
COVERAGE: THERMAL UTILITIES, CAPTIVE POWER PLANTS, COGEN UNITS, BOP ISLANDS

Structural & High-Pressure Piping Fabrication

IBR & ASME STANDARDS

SD Enterprises operates specialized shop and field fabrication capabilities for heavy structural steel and supercritical high-pressure piping. Adhering to Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR) and ASME Section IX, our certified fabrication teams deliver defect-free piping spools, turbine platforms, pipe racks, ducting, and tanks with 100% radiographic weld clearance.

High-Pressure Steam Piping

  • Main steam (MS), hot reheat (HRH), and cold reheat (CRH) spool fabrication
  • Alloy steel welding: P91, P22, P11, and carbon/stainless steels
  • Induction pre-heating and Post-Weld Heat Treatment (PWHT) monitoring
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing up to 1.5x design pressure

Heavy Structural Steel

  • Turbine hall operating floor platforms, stairways & access galleries
  • Pipe racks, equipment support trestles, and crane gantry girders
  • High-strength bolted joints and full-penetration butt weld connections
  • Industrial shot blasting, epoxy primer, and polyurethane protective coatings

Ducting, Tanks & NDT QA

  • Flue gas, primary air (PA) and secondary air (SA) ducting fabrication
  • Metallic and fabric expansion bellows installation & damper fitting
  • Industrial storage tanks, demineralized water (DM) tanks & pressure vessels
  • 100% NDT inspection: Radiography (RT), Ultrasonic (UT) & MPI clearance
WELDERS: ASME SEC IX & IBR CERTIFIED · GTAW, SMAW, SAW PROCESSES

Plant Equipment & TG Island Erection

SUB-MILLIMETER PRECISION

From greenfield power generation blocks to industrial plant expansions, SD Enterprises executes precision equipment erection for turbo-generator sets, balance of plant pumps, condensers, and heavy rotating equipment. We manage synchronized heavy crane rigging, sole plate precision leveling, laser coupling alignment, and pre-commissioning rolling trials.

TG Island Heavy Rigging

  • Turbine casing and generator stator heavy tandem crane lifts
  • Foundation sole plate placement, chemical anchoring & leveling (±0.05 mm)
  • Bearing pedestal positioning & catenary curve matching
  • Rotor threading and insertion with specialized lifting rigs

Condenser & Auxiliaries Erection

  • Surface condenser shell erection, expansion joints & neck welding
  • LP & HP feedwater heaters, deaerator, and drain cooler placement
  • Boiler feed pump (BFP) skids, booster pumps & lube oil console erection
  • Condensate extraction pumps (CEP) & cooling water pump installation

Alignment & Pre-Commissioning

  • Sub-micron dual laser alignment across all turbine-generator couplings
  • Lube oil, jacking oil, and seal oil pipe flushing & chemical pickling
  • Barring gear motor alignment & continuous rolling verification
  • Vibration signature baseline logging prior to commercial handover
SCOPE: UTILITY TG ISLANDS, CAPTIVE POWER PLANTS, COGEN, CHEMICAL & STEEL COMPLEXES
