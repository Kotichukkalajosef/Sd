The SD Story: Born On The Turbine Deck.

In 2017, a veteran group of turbine commissioning superintendents and master millwrights—having spent over two decades executing capital overhauls across thermal utilities—identified a chronic problem plaguing power plant chief engineers:

During scheduled plant shutdowns, power stations were forced to choose between two unacceptable extremes: either pay exorbitant OEM mobilization fees with rigid, slow-moving bureaucratic delays, or rely on local unorganized labor contractors who lacked micrometer measurement discipline, resulting in bearing wipeouts, catastrophic vibration trips, and missed restart deadlines.

SD Enterprises was established to bridge this exact gap. We united OEM-level metrology, calibrated precision tooling, and strict Quality Assurance Plans (QAP) with the high-speed mobilization and honest economics of a specialized engineering contractor. Every outage is executed with micro-millimeter accountability and a single goal: synchronizing your machine ahead of schedule with verified baseline vibration levels.

Milestones of Continuous Growth.

From an emergency in-situ field squad to a specialized mechanical outage contractor executing supercritical STG overhauls, here is how our capabilities evolved since 2017.

2017

Inception & Field Squads

Founded by senior turbine field specialists. Mobilized emergency flying squads for in-situ journal scraping, valve seat lapping, and rotor alignment across thermal and captive power plants.

2020

Utility STG & TDBFP Expansion

Expanded into 210 MW, 250 MW, and 500 MW utility turbine outages. Commissioned specialized hydraulic extraction tooling, achieving 12-day cartridge turnarounds for boiler feed pump turbines (BFPT).

2023

Heavy Erection & Fabrication

Expanded mechanical field capabilities into heavy turbine island equipment erection, structure alignment, optical valve lapping, and specialized shop/field piping fabrication.

PRESENT

660-800 MW Supercritical Execution

Surpassed 150+ STG outages across India, specializing in Toshiba and BHEL supercritical turbine overhauls across key NTPC generating stations (Kudgi, Darlipalli, Meja / Varanasi region) with an unblemished zero-LTI safety record.

Four Uncompromising Principles.

These non-negotiable rules govern every technician, crane lift, and dial gauge reading across all our sites.

01

Safety Before Schedule

No deadline is worth an injury. Electrical Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO), steam isolation permits, and certified rigging tackle are non-negotiable prerequisites.

02

Precision You Can Verify

We never leave measurements to memory. Every axial float, bump check, and journal clearance is recorded jointly with client engineers and documented in the handover binder.

03

Honest Scope Accountability

When unexpected erosion, blade rubbing, or casing distortion is uncovered upon opening, we diagnose immediately with practical remedies and honest engineering advice.

04

Respect For Your Restart Date

We plan critical-path schedules backwards from the grid synchronization hour, allocating round-the-clock shift resources accordingly to ensure on-time commercial operation.
